AI Sphere

The bezel is shared among your AI providers. Each arc shows what is left of its most-used quota: full and green at the start, it shrinks and turns red as you use it up. In the centre, the time and the day's basics.

Sample quotas based on CodexBar output from 11 November. Steps, temperature and battery are made-up values. The time is real.

How to read it

  • Outer arc: the percentage left in each provider's most-used window. At 100 % the arc is full, and it empties as you go. The order is fixed, so each provider is always in the same place.

  • White notch: the pace CodexBar calculates, that is, where you would be if you used the quota at a steady rate until the reset. If the arc does not reach the notch, you are using it faster than it lasts. It only appears for providers that report this (today, Codex and Claude).

  • Thin inner line: what is left in the provider's second window (for example, 5 h versus weekly).

  • Lines under the time: they only appear when there is something to watch, that is, a quota with 40 % or less left or one that will run out before its reset. At most two are shown, with what is left and the time until the reset (↻15h). Those that run out first go on top, in red, with out in 3d. If everything is fine, it just reads all quotas OK.

  • Footer: when the latest data arrived. With no connection the values are kept and the notice turns amber; a quota whose reset has already passed is drawn dotted instead of dropping to zero.

Porting it to Monkey C

The arcs are drawn with dc.drawArc and the curved text with dc.drawRadialText (vector fonts, available on the Epix Pro).

Steps, battery and temperature come from ActivityMonitor, System.getSystemStats and Weather. The time's typeface will need converting to a watch font.

The watch face does not respond to taps: whatever does not fit on the bezel is grouped, not navigated.
